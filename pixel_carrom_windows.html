PIXEL CARROM

A simple carrom-board game for Windows — aim, set your power, and pocket the coins.

Match

Turn: Player 1

Player 1 (white): 0

Player 2 (black): 0

Coins remaining: 19

Controls

Mouse: move to aim, click and drag backward from striker to set shot direction, release to shoot.

Keyboard: A/D or ←/→ aim, W/S or ↑/↓ power, Space/Enter shoot.

Pocket a coin to score. Pocketing your own color earns 1 point. Queen earns 3 points. This is a casual local two-player version.
